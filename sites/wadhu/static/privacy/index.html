Privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Wadhu (“Tools · Apps · More”) is a product brand of GOTIGIN SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PRIVATE LIMITED (“Gotigin”, “we”). This policy covers Wadhu websites (wadhu.com), Wadhu Connect (connect.wadhu.com), SMS Gateway / SMS Relay, and related Wadhu tools.

What we collect

  • Account / contact — email or phone you give us for support or future wadhu.com cloud signup.
  • Connect (messaging bridge) — WhatsApp Business Account IDs, phone number IDs, and access tokens after you complete Embedded Signup; webhook payloads Meta sends to us (messages, statuses) to operate the service.
  • SMS Gateway / Relay — message metadata needed to send SMS on your behalf; see in-app disclosures. Local LAN/Bluetooth use stays on your devices.
  • Site analytics — only if enabled on wadhu.com (optional Firebase Analytics); can be omitted on deploy.

How we use Platform / Meta data

For Wadhu Connect we use Meta permissions only to send and receive WhatsApp messages, manage templates / WABA linkage, and process webhooks after you (or your business admin) grant access. We do not sell this data or use it for unrelated advertising.

Who we share with

Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API) processes messaging when you use Connect. Hosting providers process server logs. We do not sell personal data.

Retention & deletion

Connection tokens and webhook logs are kept while the connection is active and for a limited period for abuse / debugging. To request deletion, email mearaj@gotigin.com with the subject “Wadhu data deletion”.
