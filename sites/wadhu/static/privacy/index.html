Summary

  • Local-first SMS: LAN and Bluetooth use of SMS Gateway keeps message content and API tokens on your devices. We do not need a wadhu.com account for local send.
  • Optional cloud: Hosted SMS Relay or multi-tenant Connect may process data on our (or your self-hosted) servers only when you choose those paths.
  • No sale of personal data. We do not sell personal information or use Wadhu product data for third-party advertising.
  • Deletion: see Data deletion.

Data we process (by product)

Brand site (wadhu.com)

  • Pages you request; standard server / hosting logs (IP, user agent, time).
  • Optional analytics only if enabled on a given deploy (can be omitted).
  • Email or phone you send us for support.
  • wadhu.com account (only if you register on /account): the email address or phone number you sign in with, your display name, and, if you use Google, Microsoft, or GitHub sign-in, the account id and basic profile that provider shares. Sign-in is run by Firebase Authentication (Google). An account is for hosted features; local tools do not need one.

SMS Gateway (com.wadhu.smsgateway)

Typical data on the device you install:

  • Gateway settings (listen address, paired clients, Bluetooth state)
  • API tokens you generate for LAN / Bluetooth clients
  • Outbound SMS queue / history the app keeps for operation and debugging
  • Message bodies and destination numbers you (or a client you authorised) submit for sending
  • Android permission state for SMS / network / Bluetooth as required to operate the gateway

What leaves the device

  • SMS payloads go to the mobile carrier / Android telephony stack when you send.
  • LAN or Bluetooth clients on networks you control receive API responses you authorised.
  • If you enable SMS Relay, message metadata and bodies needed to send are transmitted to that relay endpoint (yours or a Gotigin-hosted relay you opted into).

Uninstalling the Android app or clearing app storage removes on-device gateway data for that install. Export or note tokens before you wipe a device.

SMS Relay

When you run or use a relay: API keys, paired gateway identifiers, and message jobs required to deliver SMS. Self-hosted relays are under your control; Gotigin-hosted relays retain data only as needed to provide the service and for short-term abuse / debugging (see Retention).

Wadhu Connect (WhatsApp Cloud API bridge)

After Embedded Signup / Login for Business:

  • WhatsApp Business Account IDs, phone number IDs, business IDs
  • Access tokens (stored server-side to call Meta Graph APIs)
  • Webhook payloads Meta sends (messages, delivery statuses, account updates) so we can operate the bridge for your brand apps
  • Operational logs (errors, session events) for debugging

We use Meta permissions only to send and receive WhatsApp messages, manage templates / WABA linkage, and process webhooks after you (or your business admin) grant access.

Permissions (SMS Gateway on Android)

Depending on build and features, the app may request:

  • SMS-related permissions to send (and, if enabled for a default-SMS or approved Play path, read/receive) messages on the device SIM
  • Internet / network for LAN API and optional relay
  • Bluetooth for nearby client pairing
  • Notifications (if used) for send status

Play distribution of SMS capabilities is subject to Google Play SMS/Call Log policy (default handler and/or approved exception). Sideload builds may differ; the privacy commitments in this file still apply.

Security

We use HTTPS for public Connect and site traffic, store Connect tokens on the server side (not in the Embedded Signup browser page as the app secret), and verify Meta webhook signatures when configured. No method is perfect; protect device PINs, API tokens, and backups as you would a cash box.

International transfers

Servers for wadhu.com / connect.wadhu.com may be located outside India (depending on hosting). Where we transfer personal data, we do so to provide the service you requested and under applicable law (including India’s DPDP Act, 2023).

Children

Wadhu products are for businesses and developers. They are not directed at children under 18 (or the digital age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold, or withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. See Data deletion and Contact below.
