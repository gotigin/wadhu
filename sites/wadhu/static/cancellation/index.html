Wadhu cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Relationship to Gotigin

This policy is additional to the company policy at gotigin.com/cancellation.

Wadhu (Tools · Apps · More) is a brand of GOTIGIN SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PRIVATE LIMITED. The Gotigin cancellation and refund policy is the baseline. This page adds Wadhu-specific detail. Read both. Where this page conflicts with the company policy on Wadhu paid use, this page controls for that Wadhu purchase — except that mandatory law and the Gotigin company floor on prepaid terms and optional renewals still apply unless law requires otherwise.

What Wadhu sells (and what it does not)

Path Cancellation / refund note
Local / free use (e.g. SMS Gateway on your LAN or Bluetooth without hosted fees) Nothing to cancel with us for that local use; uninstall or stop using at any time
Optional hosted Wadhu services (account, relay, Connect, or other paid cloud) Prepaid term rules below; same commercial idea as Gotigin
App store listing fees / store billing That store’s refund rules for that channel

Open-source licences for crates remain as stated in each package; licence grants are not “subscriptions” and are not refunded as SaaS fees.

Hosted / paid Wadhu services

Unless mandatory law requires otherwise:

  • Fees for a current paid hosted period are not undone by self-serve mid-term cancel or automatic refund.
  • You retain access for that paid period (subject to the Wadhu Terms).
  • Next renewal is never forced; you may stop renewal so a later period is not billed.

Product pages

Individual Wadhu products may publish further cancellation pages on their own hosts (for example Connect at connect.wadhu.com/cancellation). Those are additional on top of this brand policy.

Exceptions

Email support@gotigin.com with the Wadhu product name, account or receipt details, and what you need. You will be heard; outcomes are case by case unless law or the payment partner requires a specific result.

Changes

Posted at wadhu.com/cancellation; date above updates when the text changes.
