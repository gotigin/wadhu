About Wadhu

Wadhu means more in Gujarati (and the same idea in Kutchi). The English line on our mark — Tools · Apps · More — is that promise.

Gotigin is the company behind Wadhu. In Memon it means find it, said go-tee-geen. Slogan: Where Technology Meets Discovery.

Wadhu is Gotigin’s home for reusable tools other Gotigin brands (and anyone else) can use: SMS Gateway, SMS Relay, Wadhu Connect, and more crates and apps.

Products

  • SMS Gateway — Android-first SIM messaging; LAN/Bluetooth free forever.
  • Wadhu Connect — WhatsApp Cloud API Tech Provider bridge at connect.wadhu.com.
