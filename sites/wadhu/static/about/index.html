What Wadhu is

Wadhu means more in Memon. It is Gotigin’s brand for reusable tools, utils, and apps — crates, programs, and services that other Gotigin brands (and anyone else) can use. The tagline is Tools · Apps · More.

Wadhu is the brand. It is not the company name, and it is not a substitute for the Gotigin trademark. SMS Gateway, SMS Relay, and Wadhu Connect are products that ship under it.

Products

  • SMS Gateway — Android-first SIM messaging; LAN / Bluetooth free forever (com.wadhu.smsgateway)
  • SMS Relay — optional internet plane to paired gateways
  • Wadhu Connect — WhatsApp Cloud API Tech Provider bridge at connect.wadhu.com
