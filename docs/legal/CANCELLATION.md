[gotigin.com/cancellation](https://gotigin.com/cancellation).

**GOTIGIN SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PRIVATE LIMITED**. The Gotigin cancellation and

and the Gotigin company floor on prepaid terms and optional renewals still
apply unless law requires otherwise.

## What Wadhu sells (and what it does not)

| Path | Cancellation / refund note |
| --- | --- |
| Local / free use (e.g. SMS Gateway on your LAN or Bluetooth without hosted fees) | Nothing to cancel with us for that local use; uninstall or stop using at any time |
| Optional **hosted** Wadhu services (account, relay, Connect, or other paid cloud) | Prepaid term rules below; same commercial idea as Gotigin |
| App store listing fees / store billing | That store’s refund rules for that channel |

Open-source licences for crates remain as stated in each package; licence
grants are not “subscriptions” and are not refunded as SaaS fees.

## Hosted / paid Wadhu services

Unless mandatory law requires otherwise:

- Fees for a **current** paid hosted period are not undone by self-serve
  mid-term cancel or automatic refund.
- You retain access for that paid period (subject to the Wadhu Terms).
- **Next** renewal is never forced; you may stop renewal so a later period
  is not billed.

## Product pages

Individual Wadhu products may publish **further** cancellation pages on their

[connect.wadhu.com/cancellation](https://connect.wadhu.com/cancellation)).
Those are **additional on top of this brand policy**.

## Exceptions

Email [support@gotigin.com](mailto:support@gotigin.com) with the Wadhu product
name, account or receipt details, and what you need. You will be heard;
outcomes are case by case unless law or the payment partner requires a
specific result.
